EXECUTIVE VERDICT
=================

The plan is not executable as written; proceed only after amendments, not as-is.
The single biggest risk is immediate: both planned Groq models were retired on July 17, 2026.
The freshness design will classify pages incorrectly because it hashes fetched HTML against
extracted text.

mostly fixed and the demo calls are local.

failures or report evidence collection.

Fix F01-F16 before code; explicitly de-scope non-rubric features if the extension is not confirmed.

BUSINESS-CASE CORRECTION
========================

The differentiation is genuine only if provenance and freshness operate per factual claim.
Structured field-level answers, prerequisite-document resolution, explicit unknowns, and a content
review queue enable a different decision from generic Q&A: whether a citizen is ready to visit and
whether OMSAR should trust or review the underlying content.

The weakest point is evidence. SCOPE.md §1 assumes two-to-three visits, four hours per failed visit,
+20% first-visit completion, and deflection value without measured or cited baselines. The project
also lacks a named organizational owner of the review queue.

Recommended pitch:

"An auditable preparation and content-quality layer for Dawlati that turns procedure pages into
source-linked checklists and routes incomplete or changed content to OMSAR reviewers."

Present citizens as users, OMSAR content operations as the system owner, and first-visit readiness
plus reviewer correction rate as outcomes. Add a small documented comparison against the existing
assistant/aggregators, and label team assumptions explicitly.

backup video.

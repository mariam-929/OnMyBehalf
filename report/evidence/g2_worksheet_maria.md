> already cost Ali an afternoon.

3. Find the service by its Arabic title below and expand it.

> **Don't** use the `dawlati.gov.lb/ministry_service_ser/...` links you may see elsewhere in
> the project — those individual pages are genuinely blank. All the content lives inside the
> guide page above. (That's a real finding we report in the paper, not a mistake on our side.)

**What you write:** next to each numbered document, replace `______` with one of:

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| `OK` | the site really does list this document, and we captured it correctly |
| `WRONG` | the wording is wrong / it says something different |
| `NOT A DOCUMENT` | this is a note, an instruction, or a location — not a required document |
| `SPLIT` | **two separate documents got glued into one line** — then write them out separately under MISSING |

**The single most valuable thing you can find** is a document the website lists that is
**not in our list at all**. Those go under **MISSING**. A missing document means the agent
would tell a citizen to show up at the office without it.
